Features

Treasure route

  • Knows your professions. You only see treasures for the professions your character has learned.
  • Builds a short route. It starts from where you're standing and works through the zones in an efficient order.
  • Points you to each treasure. It sets the in-game waypoint arrow, and works with TomTom.
  • Knows the portals. When the next treasure is in another zone, it tells you which portal to take and puts the waypoint on the portal.
  • Moves on by itself. Loot a treasure and the waypoint jumps to the next one. Enter a new zone and it jumps to the nearest treasure there.
  • Tracks progress. See how many treasures you've collected for each profession.
  • Has notes for tricky treasures. Each one has a short tip, including which are hidden by phasing or need a quest or achievement first.

Covers Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Herbalism, Inscription, Jewelcrafting, Leatherworking, Mining, Skinning and Tailoring in Silvermoon City, Eversong Woods, Zul'Aman, Atal'Aman, Voidstorm and Slayer's Rise, and Harandar and The Den.

Darkmoon Faire helper

Each month the Darkmoon Faire has a quest worth +2 knowledge for every profession. When you arrive on Darkmoon Island, the helper opens by itself and shows the quests for your professions:

  • Whether each quest is Available or Done this Faire.
  • The vendor's name and roughly where they stand.
  • What to bring with you, and what the vendor gives you.
  • Numbered steps from accepting the quest to turning it in.

Use the Prev and Next buttons at the bottom to switch between your professions.

Minimizing

Press the minimize button in the tracker's header and it shrinks to a slim bar with your next treasure, its zone and coordinates, and how many are left. Use the arrow on the bar to skip ahead, or press expand to bring back the full tracker.

The Darkmoon Faire helper can be minimized too. The bar then shows only the step you're on, such as "Step 2 of 5". Use the arrows to move forward or back. Going past the last step moves on to your next profession's quest.

Looks

The first time you log in, a window asks how you want the addon to look. Every window changes to match.

  • Artisan's Ledger: warm walnut and brass with engraved borders, like a craftsman's notebook.
  • Horde: the Artisan's Ledger in blood red and iron.
  • Alliance: the Artisan's Ledger in royal blue and gold.
  • Workbench: flat and modern. Each profession gets its own color.
  • Slate: clean black, grey and gold, matching Lixard's other addons.

Installing

The easiest way is the CurseForge app: search for Profession Knowledge Treasures and press Install.

To install by hand:

  1. Download the zip from CurseForge or from GitHub (Code > Download ZIP).
  2. Copy the ProfessionKnowledgeTreasures folder into World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns.
  3. Restart the game the first time.

Getting started

  1. Log in and pick a look in the window that appears. You can change it later.
  2. The tracker opens if you have treasures left to collect. Follow the waypoint arrow to the first treasure and loot it.
  3. The waypoint moves to the next treasure on its own. Keep going until the tracker tells you you're done. It closes by itself once everything is collected.

Professions not detected? Type /pkt profs and pick them yourself. You can choose up to two, and the choice is saved for that character. Press Clear in the same window to go back to automatic detection.

Settings

Open settings with the button in the tracker's header, by middle-clicking the minimap button, or by typing /pkt settings.

Appearance
Shows your current look. Press Change look to pick another.
Waypoint system
Native + TomTom sets both arrows. Native only uses the game's arrow. TomTom only uses only that arrow. None (map pin only) places a map pin with no arrow.
Professions
Press Select manually to pick your professions if they aren't detected.

Tips and FAQ

I'm standing on the spot but there's no treasure.

Some treasures are hidden by phasing until you finish a storyline, quest or achievement, and the tip in the tracker says which one. Several treasures in Atal'Aman only appear after the local storyline.

Two treasures point to the same place.

A few treasures share a spot. In Zul'Aman, the Alchemy and Enchanting treasures sit on the same cart.

The addon moved my quest tracking arrow.

Right after a loading screen, the addon takes the arrow back for the treasure route. If you track a quest yourself after that, it stays tracked. Press Next, Prev, Nearest or First to point the arrow back at a treasure.

The tracker shows the wrong professions, or none.

Type /pkt profs and pick up to two professions. Press Clear in that window to go back to automatic detection.

The Darkmoon Faire helper didn't open.

It opens on its own when you arrive on Darkmoon Island. You can open it any time with /pkt dmf or by right-clicking the minimap button.

How often do the Darkmoon Faire quests reset?

Every month, when the Faire returns. Each one gives +2 knowledge for that profession.
