In development

Trailmark is in development and not released yet. It changes almost every day, so this page gives an overview instead of every setting. Names and options may still move around.

Features

The guide window

  • Three leveling styles: Everything (every step of every zone, side quests and grey quests too), Main quest line (the same without side quests) and Fastest leveling (quests near your level in the quickest zone order for your race and class). The style is set per character.
  • Written routes: every zone has its own route for Alliance and Horde, followed top to bottom. Quests you do in one place are shown together as a block.
  • Next zone: when a zone is done, the window offers the next one. When a zone's main quests are done, it shows the side quests left at your level.
  • Step tools: right-click a step to mark it done, skip it, restart from it, point the waypoint at it or abandon its quest. Hover a step to see why it's waiting.
  • Also in your log: quests you carry that the route isn't doing right now. Click one to follow it.
  • Five looks: Cartographer, Quest Log, Waystone, Grove and Forge.

Finding the way

  • Waypoint: when Blizzard shows a quest marker for the step, Trailmark tracks that quest so you follow Blizzard's own marker. If Blizzard's marker for a hand-in points far away from the quest's hand-in NPC, Trailmark pins the NPC instead. Other steps get Trailmark's map pin. You can use an in-world marker or a screen arrow.
  • Travel planner: for far steps it plans the way through flight paths you know, your hearthstone, boats, zeppelins, the tram and class teleports, and shows the plan in a Getting there panel. Flight times follow the real flight routes with all their stops, so a long flight with many stops doesn't win over a shorter flight and a boat. On the flight map, the flight to take is marked.
  • Route line through your next steps on the world map.
  • Go to the nearest repair, food and drink, vendor, innkeeper, flight master, class trainer, bank or mailbox.

Less busywork

  • Accept guide quests and turn in finished ones for you (hold Shift to stop it), pick the best reward and wear upgrades.
  • The guide keeps going in combat: the waypoint moves on as soon as you finish an objective, and auto accept, turn-in and quest sharing still work. Only what the game blocks in combat waits: the use button, equipping gear, opening containers and learning talents.
  • Take the guide's flight, skip the chat at flight masters, vendors and trainers, and buy items a quest asks for.
  • Sell grey items, repair, restock food, drink, ammo and pet food, and open clams and other containers.
  • A target button and a quest item button next to the window, quest progress over mobs, and a warning before a long stretch when your bags are full or your gear is worn.
  • A reminder when your class trainer has new spells (counting only spells you can learn now), and a Train all button.

Talents

Leveling and level 60 talent builds for every class, updated from the current WoW Forever guides, including a Bear tank leveling build for druids (see the talent review for how each build was chosen). A panel sits beside the talent window, the next talents are marked on the tree, and a reminder shows when you have points to spend. Trailmark can place the points for you, or you click Learn yourself.

Tabs

  • Journey: time to your next level and to 60, your road to 60, this zone's progress and what's coming up (talents, trainer, riding, flight paths).
  • Dungeons: dungeons for your level with their quests. Pick up all opens a guide that takes you to every quest giver, then the entrance, then the turn-ins.
  • Professions: profession leveling plans.
  • Reputation: reputation plans per faction.
  • Explore: areas you haven't explored yet.
  • Legacy: Legacy challenges and points.
  • Party: party members with Trailmark see each other's guide and step, and can share quests the others are missing.

Recorder and shared routes

The recorder saves your quests, flights, hearth and notes while you play. Turn a recording into a route code and send it to a friend; their code is added under Shared routes in the guide list.

Installing

There's no public download yet. Once Trailmark is released, install it with the CurseForge app: search for Trailmark.

To install from a zip, copy the Trailmark folder into World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns.

Restart the game the first time, since a reload doesn't load new addons.

Getting started

  1. Log in. A short setup opens after a moment: pick a leveling style, a talent build, how the way is shown and which helpers to turn on.
  2. Press Finish. Trailmark loads a zone that fits your character and, if you ticked it, a short tour of the window starts.
  3. Type /trail guides to pick another zone.
  4. Type /trail to show or hide the window. Left-click the gear for settings, right-click it for the quick menu.

Run the setup again any time with /trail setup.

Tips and FAQ

How do I stop auto accept for one quest?

Hold Shift while you talk to the NPC.

Can I change the leveling style later?

Yes, on the Leveling settings page. It's saved per character.

A step is wrong. How do I tell you?

Right-click the step and pick Report this step. /trail reports lists what you saved.

When will it be released?

When testing on WoW Forever is done.
