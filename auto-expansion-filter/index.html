Auto Expansion Filter

Stop wading through old-expansion items. Auto Expansion Filter turns on "Current Expansion Only" for you every time you open the Auction House or the Crafting Orders browser.

Download on CurseForge Source on GitHub
  • Command: /aef
  • Game: Retail, Midnight
  • Setup: none needed

What it does

  • Auction House: every time the search bar appears, "Current Expansion Only" is turned on in its Filter menu.
  • Crafting Orders: the same filter is turned on when you browse customer orders.
  • Separate switches: turn each one on or off on its own.
  • Reliable: it checks a few times as the window opens, because the game doesn't always accept the change on the first try.

It doesn't change any other window or filter.

Getting started

  1. Install the addon and log in.
  2. Open the Auction House or browse Crafting Orders. "Current Expansion Only" is already on.

That's it. Both filters are on from the start, and your settings apply to every character on your account.

Settings

Type /aef to open the settings window. It shows the addon version under the title. Drag it to move it, and press Close or Escape to close it.

/autofilter and /expfilter work the same as /aef.

Tips and FAQ

I unticked the filter in the Auction House, but it came back.

The addon sets the filter every time the window opens, so a change you make by hand lasts until the next time you open it. To keep it off, untick that window in /aef.

What happens when I turn a switch off?

The addon then turns "Current Expansion Only" off each time that window opens, instead of on.

It says "Config not loaded yet".

You typed the command before the game finished loading. Wait a moment and try again.
