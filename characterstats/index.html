User guide

CharacterStats

Keep your item level and stats on screen without opening the character window. CharacterStats adds a small stat panel you can place anywhere, a stat list and gear slot details in the character window, and a quick way to share your stats in chat.

Download on CurseForge Source on GitHub
  • Command: /cs
  • Game: Retail, Mists of Pandaria Classic and WoW Classic Forever
  • Languages: 12

Preview

Features

  • An on-screen stat panel showing item level, primary and secondary stats, defensive and utility stats, and movement speed. Move it anywhere, lock it, and scale it.
  • Stats in the character window, right next to your gear.
  • Gear slot details: item level colored by upgrade track, upgrade level (4/6), enchants and gems, and flags for missing enchants and empty sockets. You choose where each one sits.
  • A wider character window that gives those details room next to your character.
  • Diminishing returns on hover, on the Meters bars and, if you like, on item tooltips.
  • Four looks for the panel and the character window, each one previewed live before you pick.
  • Three window themes for the options window and popups: Classic, Workbench and Artisan Ledger.
  • Sharing: right-click the panel to post your stats to your raid, party, target or a Battle.net friend.
  • Profiles, including automatic switching when you change specialization.
  • Pick your stats: turn any stat on or off, drag them into your own order, and recolor them.
  • Your language: show the addon in any of its 12 languages, whatever language your game uses.
  • Plays nicely with other addons: it can notice when another addon also changes the character window and let you choose which one to keep.

Getting started

  1. Install the addon and log in. Chat shows "CharacterStats loaded. Type /cs to toggle."
  2. The stat panel appears on screen. Drag it with the left mouse button to where you want it.
  3. Optional: type /cs style to pick a different look. Click a look to preview it live, then press Use This Look.
  4. Open the options with /cs config or left-click the minimap button. The gear slot details and the other extras are on the Character Frame page.

Minimap button: left-click opens the options, right-click shows or hides the stat panel, and shift-click puts the panel back in its default position.

The four looks

Change your look any time with /cs style or in Appearance in the options.

Original

The classic list from earlier versions.

Ledger

A big item level, with stats grouped under headers.

Meters

A colored bar under every stat.

Companion

Compact cells, plus a gear drawer on the character window with Stats, Gear and Ratings tabs.

The character window

Open your character window and you'll see your stats listed beside your gear. The window is 50 pixels wider than Blizzard's to give the gear details room next to your character. Set it back to 0 under Character Frame if you prefer the original size.

Turn on the gear slot details you want on the Character Frame page:

  • Item level on each piece, colored by its upgrade track from Explorer to Myth, plus Crafted.
  • Upgrade level, such as 4/6, or 4/6 Hero with the track name. On Mists of Pandaria Classic it shows upgrades like 1/2.
  • Enchants and gems next to each slot, as a quality icon (hover for the name) or as the enchant's name.
  • Missing enchant notes on slots that should be enchanted, and a dot for empty gem sockets.

Each part can sit on the icon or beside it, at the top, middle or bottom, on the side facing your character. Item level and upgrade level each have their own size and position on the icon.

The details are off until you turn them on.

Diminishing returns

Secondary stats lose value past certain amounts. CharacterStats shows where you stand:

  • Hover critical strike, haste, mastery, versatility, leech, avoidance or speed on the stat panel or in the character window to see your current penalty, the rating until the next penalty, and your effective rating.
  • In the Meters look, the bars fill up to the point where the penalty starts, then a darker shade shows how far past it you are.
  • Turn on item tooltips under General to see what each stat on an item is worth after diminishing returns.
  • The Ratings tab in the Companion drawer shows the rating you need for 1% more at your current penalty.

Sharing your stats

Right-click the stat panel to open the Share menu, then choose where to post: Raid, Party, your current target as a whisper, or a Battle.net friend. Stats the game is currently hiding are left out of what you share.

Tips and FAQ

Some of my stats show a dash in combat.

In Midnight the game hides some values during combat. CharacterStats never guesses at hidden numbers, so it shows a dash until the game makes them available again.

Why is my Versatility lower than the character sheet?

CharacterStats shows the versatility from your rating, live in combat. Flat bonuses such as Mark of the Wild are left out because the game hides them from addons in combat.

I can't open the options.

The options window, the look picker and the Share menu can't open during combat. Try again once combat ends.

Another addon also changes my character window.

Addons like ElvUI or EllesmereUI can overlap with CharacterStats. Turn on Check for Conflicting Character Frame Addons and it asks whether to turn off CharacterStats' part, turn off the other addon, or keep both.

Does it work on Classic?

Yes. On Mists of Pandaria Classic the character window lists Blizzard's own stat categories for your class and spec, with stats such as Spirit, Hit and Expertise. On WoW Classic Forever you also get health, power, weapon damage, spell healing, penetration and resistances. Gear slot details and the wider window work on both.
