CharacterStats

Keep your item level and stats on screen without opening the character window. CharacterStats adds a small stat panel you can place anywhere, a stat list and gear badges in the character window, and a quick way to share your stats in chat.

Download on CurseForge
  • Command: /cs
  • Game: Retail and Mists of Pandaria Classic
  • Languages: 12

Features

  • An on-screen stat panel showing item level, primary and secondary stats, defensive and utility stats, and movement speed. Move it anywhere and lock it in place.
  • Stats in the character window, right next to your gear.
  • Gear badges showing each item's level, colored by upgrade track from Explorer to Myth, plus Crafted. They also flag missing enchants and empty gem sockets.
  • Four looks to choose from, each one previewed live before you pick.
  • Sharing: right-click the panel to post your stats to your raid, party, target or a Battle.net friend.
  • Profiles, including automatic switching when you change specialization.
  • Pick your stats: turn any stat on or off, drag them into your own order, and recolor them.
  • Plays nicely with other addons: it notices when another addon also changes the character window and lets you choose which one to keep.

Getting started

  1. Install the addon and log in. Chat shows "CharacterStats loaded. Type /cs to toggle."
  2. A window opens asking you to choose a look. Click a look to preview it live, then press Use This Look. Pick Later closes it for now.
  3. The stat panel appears on screen. Drag it with the left mouse button to where you want it.
  4. Open the options with /cs config, right-click the minimap button, or click the options button on the character window.

Minimap button: left-click shows or hides the stat panel, right-click opens options, and shift-click puts the panel back in its default position.

The four looks

Change your look any time with /cs style or in Appearance in the options.

Original

The classic list from earlier versions.

Ledger

A big item level, with stats grouped under headers.

Meters

A colored bar under every stat.

Companion

Compact cells, plus a gear drawer on the character window with Stats, Gear and Ratings tabs.

The character window

Open your character window and you'll see your stats listed beside your gear, and badges on each gear slot:

  • The item level of each piece, colored by its upgrade track.
  • A dot when a piece is missing an enchant or has an empty gem socket.
  • Your enchants and gems shown next to each slot, as a quality icon (hover for the name) or as the enchant's name.

Everything here can be turned off in the Character Frame page of the options.

Sharing your stats

Right-click the stat panel to open the Share menu, then choose where to post: Raid, Party, your current target as a whisper, or a Battle.net friend. Stats the game is currently hiding are left out of what you share.

Tips and FAQ

Some of my stats show a dash in combat.

In Midnight the game hides some values during combat. CharacterStats never guesses at hidden numbers, so it shows a dash until the game makes them available again.

Why is Versatility an estimate in Mythic+ and Delves?

The game restricts that value in those places, so the addon shows its best estimate instead.

I can't open the options.

The options window, the look picker and the Share menu can't open during combat. Try again once combat ends.

Another addon also changes my character window.

Addons like ElvUI or EllesmereUI can overlap with CharacterStats. When it notices one, it asks whether to turn off CharacterStats' part, turn off the other addon, or keep both.

Does it work on Classic?

Yes, on Mists of Pandaria Classic, with the stats that matter there such as Spirit, Hit and Expertise. The missing-enchant check is retail only.
