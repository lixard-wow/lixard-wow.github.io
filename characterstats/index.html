CharacterStats

Keep your item level and stats on screen without opening the character window. CharacterStats adds a small stat panel you can place anywhere, a stat list and gear slot details in the character window, and a quick way to share your stats in chat.

Features

The stat panel

  • An on-screen stat panel with item level, primary and secondary stats, defensive and utility stats, and movement speed. Move it anywhere, lock it, and scale it.
  • Four looks: Original, Ledger, Meters and Companion. Each one is previewed live before you pick it.
  • Pick your stats: turn any stat on or off, drag them into your own order, and recolor them.
  • Sharing: right-click the panel to post your stats to your raid, party, target or a Battle.net friend.

The character window

  • Stats in the character window, right next to your gear. Names are light gray and values white, or turn on per-stat colors.
  • Gear slot details: item level colored by upgrade track, upgrade level (4/6 or 4/6 Hero), enchants and gems, and flags for missing enchants and empty sockets. You choose where each one sits.
  • A wider character window that gives those details room next to your character.
  • A gear drawer with Stats, Gear and Ratings tabs when you use the Companion look.

Diminishing returns

  • Hover critical strike, haste, mastery, versatility, leech, avoidance or speed to see your current penalty, the rating until the next penalty, and your effective rating.
  • In the Meters look, bars fill up to where the penalty starts, then a darker shade shows how far past it you are.
  • Item tooltips can show what each stat on an item is worth after diminishing returns.
  • The drawer's Ratings tab shows the rating you need for 1% more at your current penalty.

Everything else

  • Profiles, with automatic switching when you change specialization.
  • Window themes for the options window and popups: Classic, Workbench, Artisan Ledger, Horde and Alliance.
  • Your language: show the addon in any of its 12 languages, whatever language your game uses.
  • Character window check: it can notice when another addon also changes the character window and let you choose which one to keep.

Installing

The easiest way is the CurseForge app: search for CharacterStats and click Install.

To install by hand:

  1. Download the zip from CurseForge or from GitHub (Code > Download ZIP).
  2. Copy the CharacterStats folder into World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns. For Mists of Pandaria Classic use _classic_, and for WoW Forever use _forever_, instead of _retail_.
  3. Restart the game the first time, so it finds the new addon.

Getting started

  1. Log in. The stat panel appears on screen. Drag it with the left mouse button to where you want it.
  2. Optional: type /cs style to pick a different look. Click a look to preview it, then click Use This Look.
  3. Open your character window to see your stats beside your gear.
  4. Open the options with /cs config or left-click the minimap button. The gear slot details and other character window extras are on the Character Frame page.

Minimap button: left-click opens the options, right-click shows or hides the stat panel, and Shift-click puts the panel back in its default position.

Tips and FAQ

Where did the colors in my character window go?

Stats in the character window are now light gray and white by default. Turn on Color Each Stat on Character Frame on the Character Frame page to color each stat again.

Some of my stats show a dash in combat.

The game hides some values from addons during combat. CharacterStats never guesses at hidden numbers, so it shows a dash until the game makes them available again.

Why is my Versatility lower than the character sheet?

CharacterStats shows the versatility from your rating, live in combat. Flat bonuses such as Mark of the Wild are left out because the game hides them from addons in combat.

I can't open the options.

The options window, the look picker and the Share menu can't open during combat. Try again once combat ends.

Another addon also changes my character window.

Turn on Check for Conflicting Character Frame Addons on the Character Frame page. It asks whether to turn off CharacterStats' part, turn off the other addon, or keep both.

My character window is wider than before.

CharacterStats adds 50 px to make room for the gear slot details. Set Extra Character Frame Width to 0 to get the original size back.
