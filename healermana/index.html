User guide

HealerMana

Know your healers' mana before the pull goes sideways. HealerMana shows the mana of every healer in your group or raid in one small display, so a glance tells you who's about to run dry.

Download on CurseForge Source on GitHub
  • Command: /hm
  • Game: Retail, Midnight
  • Works with: every healer spec

Preview

Features

  • Every healer's mana, updated live, as icons or bars.
  • Your choice of layout: a horizontal row, a vertical list or a grid.
  • Out-of-range dimming, so you can see who's too far away.
  • Class colors and spec icons for each healer.
  • Status at a glance: Dead and Offline labels, a food and drink icon while a healer is drinking, and a marker when a Druid healer is in Cat, Bear or Moonkin Form.
  • Choose where it shows: open world, dungeons, raids, scenarios, battlegrounds and arenas, each on or off.
  • Sort alphabetically or by class.
  • Move, resize and lock it. Your settings are saved.

Works with Holy and Discipline Priest, Restoration Druid, Holy Paladin, Restoration Shaman, Mistweaver Monk and Preservation Evoker.

Getting started

  1. Install the addon and log in. Chat shows "HealerMana loaded. Type /hm for options."
  2. Join a group that has a healer, including yourself if you play one. The display appears just right of the middle of your screen.
  3. Type /hm or click the minimap button to open settings. Pick icons or bars, then adjust the size, spacing and sorting.
  4. Drag the display where you want it, then turn on Lock frame so you don't move it by accident.

Want to set it up without a group? Press Start Preview at the bottom of the settings window, or type /hm raid 5, to show fake healers. /hm raid off goes back to your real group.

Reading the display

  • The number is the healer's mana as a percentage.
  • A faded healer is out of range.
  • Dead and Offline replace the percentage when they apply. A healer who is both shows Offline.
  • In icon style, a food and drink icon shows while the healer is eating or drinking.
  • A Druid healer in Cat, Bear or Moonkin Form shows that form's icon (icon style) or a [Cat], [Bear] or [Moonkin] tag (bar style). Mana is hidden in Cat and Bear Form, since they don't use it.

Settings

Open settings with /hm or the minimap button. The bottom of the window has Start Preview, Reset Position and Close.

Tips and FAQ

The food and drink icon doesn't show in Mythic+.

Blizzard blocks addons from seeing this during an entire Mythic+ run, not just during pulls. There's no way around it, and it works everywhere else.

A healer's icon is generic for a moment.

HealerMana looks up each healer's spec in the background, one player every couple of seconds. Until it knows, it uses the role they picked and a class icon. It pauses while you have someone's Inspect window open, so it never gets in your way.

The Druid Moonkin marker is slow to change.

Moonkin Form only updates outside combat. Cat and Bear Form update during combat too.

I don't see the Font or Bar Texture options.

Those lists come from a shared library that many other addons install. If none of your addons include it, HealerMana uses its default font and texture.

The display isn't showing.

It only shows when you're grouped with a healer, in content you've turned on in Sort and Visibility. Turn on Show when Solo to see it on your own.

What's new in 1.0.5

  • Fixed HealerMana's background spec lookup blocking you from inspecting other players. It now pauses while your Inspect window is open and stops once every healer's spec is known.
