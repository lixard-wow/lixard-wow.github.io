The addons

All of them work with the current retail game. Plateau is in beta and downloads from GitHub; the rest are on CurseForge. Pick one to read its guide.

Plateau Nameplates

Nameplates · Beta

Fast, readable enemy nameplates built for Midnight's addon rules, with cast bars that follow your interrupt, threat colors, and a settings window where you click and drag a live sample plate.

Read the guide GitHub

Profession Knowledge Treasures

Professions · Midnight

Routes you to every profession knowledge treasure in Midnight, moves the waypoint on as you loot, and walks you through the Darkmoon Faire quests one step at a time.

Read the guide CurseForge

CharacterStats

Character · Stats

Keeps your item level and stats in a small on-screen panel, adds a stat list and gear slot details to the character window, and lets you share your stats in chat.

Read the guide CurseForge

HealerMana

Group · Healing

Shows the mana of every healer in your group or raid in one small display, so you can see who is running low before the pull.

Read the guide CurseForge

Mark of the Tank

Hunter · Rogue

Keeps your Misdirection or Tricks of the Trade macro pointed at the right player, switching to the tank automatically when you join a group.

Read the guide CurseForge

Auto Expansion Filter

Auction House · Crafting Orders

Turns on "Current Expansion Only" for you every time you open the Auction House or the Crafting Orders browser, so old-expansion items stay out of your results.

Read the guide CurseForge

Installing an addon

The easiest way is the CurseForge app, which also keeps your addons up to date.

With the CurseForge app

Search for the addon by name in the World of Warcraft section and click Install. Updates arrive automatically.

By hand

Download the zip from the addon's CurseForge page and unzip it into World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns. For Plateau, follow the GitHub steps in its guide.

Then in game

Restart the game or type /reload, and make sure the addon is ticked in the AddOns list on the character select screen.
