World of Warcraft addons

Addons by Lixard

Free addons for retail World of Warcraft and WoW Forever, from single-job tools to a complete interface. Every one is open on GitHub; the released ones are also on CurseForge.

Installing

1CurseForge app

For released addons: search the name in the World of Warcraft section and click Install. Updates arrive by themselves.

2From GitHub

On the addon's GitHub page click Code, then Download ZIP, and copy the addon folders from the zip into _retail_\Interface\AddOns.

3In game

Restart the game the first time, then tick the addon in the AddOns list on the character screen. After updates, /reload is enough.
