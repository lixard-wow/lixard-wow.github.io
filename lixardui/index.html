In development

LixardUI is in development and not released yet. It changes almost every day, so this page gives an overview of each part instead of every setting. Names and options may still move around.

Features

Each part of LixardUI is its own module. A module that's off never loads, and Blizzard's version of that part stays.

Frames and buttons

  • Unit frames: player, target, target of target, focus, pet, boss and arena frames, with buffs and debuffs, cast bars, class resources, portraits, totem and summon timers and the encounter bar.
  • Party and raid frames: health with your HoTs, debuffs, buffs, major defensives and a dispel glow, role and leader icons, out-of-range fading, an aggro border, and tank and main assist frames. /lui grouptest shows a preview while you're alone.
  • Action bars: bars 1-8 with fading and mouseover, the game menu and bag buttons, and the extra and zone ability buttons. Point at any button and press a key to bind it.
  • Buffs and debuffs: your own buffs and debuffs, with weapon enchants. Right click a buff to cancel it.
  • Cooldown manager: Blizzard's Cooldown Manager, arranged and styled, plus countdown numbers on cooldowns.

World

  • Minimap: round or square, zone name, clock and coordinates, and one place that collects your addon buttons.
  • World map: coordinates, fading while you move, an in-world waypoint, and map pins with /way.
  • Quest tracker: your tracked quests with groups, sorting, quest item buttons, sounds, automation and a Mythic+ timer.
  • Info bars: data text bars (durability, gold, bags, time and your addons' data) and experience, reputation and honor bars.

Social

  • Chat: styled chat windows and tabs, copy chat, clickable web links and a typing box that remembers what you typed.
  • Friends list: one window for friends, messages, guild, keystones, invite lists, history, raid, who and ignore, with online alerts.
  • Tooltips and menus in the same look, with class colored names, item level and the unit's target.

Items and windows

  • Bags and bank: one window with sections, sorting, junk tools, your other characters' items and search. Alt-click an item to lock its slot, pick its section, mark it as junk, or mark it Never sell or Never destroy.
  • Vendor, mailbox and auction house windows in the LixardUI look, with auto-repair and selling junk at vendors.
  • Damage meter: damage, healing, interrupts, deaths and more from Blizzard's own damage meter, in LixardUI windows.
  • Blizzard window skins: gives Blizzard's own windows the LixardUI look, each with its own switch.

Quality of life

  • Move Blizzard windows, and Ctrl + mouse wheel to resize them.
  • An addon manager with memory and CPU use, locked addons and saved addon profiles.
  • Buff reminder, cursor finder, faster loot, summon countdown and group tools.
  • On Retail: Mythic+ helpers (insert keystone, dungeon teleports), a battle res tracker, a talent loadout reminder, crafting order helpers and a WoW Token alert.

Installing

There's no download yet. Once LixardUI is released, install it with the CurseForge app: search for LixardUI.

To install by hand, download the zip from CurseForge and copy these folders into World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns (or World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns for WoW Forever):

LixardUI, LixardUI_ActionBars, LixardUI_Auction, LixardUI_Auras, LixardUI_Bags, LixardUI_Chat, LixardUI_Cooldowns, LixardUI_DataBars, LixardUI_Friends, LixardUI_GroupFrames, LixardUI_IconData, LixardUI_Mail, LixardUI_Maps, LixardUI_Meters, LixardUI_Minimap, LixardUI_Options, LixardUI_QoL, LixardUI_Skins, LixardUI_Tooltips, LixardUI_Tracker, LixardUI_UnitFrames, LixardUI_Vendor.

Restart the game the first time, since a reload doesn't load new addons.

Getting started

  1. Log in. A short setup opens after a moment: pick a profile, a highlight color and which parts of the interface LixardUI takes over.
  2. Press Finish. If you changed modules, it reloads the interface.
  3. Type /lui any time to open the settings window. You can also open it from the addon compartment by the minimap, or from Esc > Options > AddOns.
  4. Type /lui move (or open Blizzard's Edit Mode) to move frames. Drag a box, use the arrow keys to nudge it, and click its gear for position, size and options.

Run the setup again any time with /lui setup.

Settings

The settings window is split into chapters, each with pages, sub-pages and tabs. The search box at the top finds any setting and jumps to it. Turn on Show help text at the bottom to see the explanation under each setting.

Look

The window texture, the highlight color (or Use my class color), fonts, bar textures, corners and border color everywhere, and the screen scale. Tooltips are set up here too.

Frames

Shared rules for all unit frames, then a page for each frame: player, target, focus, pet, boss, arena, party and raid.

Buttons & buffs

Action bars, your buffs and debuffs, and the Cooldown Manager.

Combat & groups

Mythic+ helpers, readiness reminders, group tools, the interrupt tracker and the damage meter.

World

Minimap, world map, quest tracker and info bars.

Social

Chat, friends, whispers, invites and history, and guild.

Items & windows

Bags and bank, when bags open, vendor and selling, auctions and crafting, mailbox, Blizzard window skins and window helpers.

System

Profiles
A profile holds how LixardUI looks and where things sit. Any character can use any profile. Create, copy, reset, delete, and export or import a profile as text.
Modules
Turn whole parts of LixardUI on or off for this character. Anything that's off uses Blizzard's own version. Changes apply after a reload.
Addon manager
Turns the addon manager on, and can make the game menu's AddOns button open it.
About
Version, game, memory and CPU use, and handy commands.

Tips and FAQ

Can I use only part of it?

Yes. Open the Modules page in the settings, or type /lui disable <module>. A module that's off never loads, and Blizzard's version of that part stays. Module choices are per character.

How do I use the same setup on another character?

On the other character, open Profiles and pick your profile under Active profile. To share it with another account or a friend, use Export and Import.

How do I put a frame back where it was?

Type /lui move and click the frame's gear for its position, or use the reset button there to put every frame back to its default spot.

Something looks wrong after an update.

Type /rl. If the update added new files, restart the game fully, since a reload doesn't load new files.

Why is a setting missing on WoW Forever?

Some helpers, such as the Mythic+ tools, talent loadout reminder and WoW Token alert, are Retail only and don't show on WoW Forever.

When will it be released?

When testing on Retail and WoW Forever is done.
