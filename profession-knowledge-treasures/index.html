Profession Knowledge Treasures

Find every profession knowledge treasure in Midnight and always know where to go next. The addon builds a route, points the waypoint at the next treasure, and moves on by itself as you loot.

  • Game: Retail, Midnight
  • Covers: 88 treasures, 11 professions

Preview

Features

  • Knows your professions. You only see treasures for the professions your character has learned.
  • Builds a short route. It starts from where you're standing and works through the zones in an efficient order.
  • Points you to each treasure. It sets the in-game waypoint arrow, and also adds a TomTom arrow if you use TomTom.
  • Knows the portals. When the next treasure is in another zone, it tells you which portal to take and puts the waypoint on the portal.
  • Moves on by itself. Loot a treasure and the waypoint jumps to the next one. Enter a new zone and it jumps to the nearest treasure there.
  • Tracks progress. See how many treasures you've collected for each profession.
  • Has notes for tricky treasures. Each one has a short tip, including which are hidden by phasing or need a quest or achievement first.
  • Includes a Darkmoon Faire helper. Everything you need for each profession's monthly +2 knowledge quest.

Zones covered: Silvermoon City, Eversong Woods, Zul'Aman, Atal'Aman, Voidstorm and Slayer's Rise, and Harandar and The Den.

Getting started

  1. Install the addon and log in.
  2. Pick a look in the window that appears. You can change it later.
  3. The tracker opens if you have treasures left to collect. Follow the waypoint arrow to the first treasure and loot it.
  4. The waypoint moves to the next treasure on its own. Keep going until the tracker tells you you're done.

Professions not detected? Type /pkt profs and pick them yourself. You can choose up to two, and the choice is saved for that character. Press Clear in the same window to go back to automatic detection.

The tracker

The tracker shows your next treasure, its zone and coordinates, a tip for finding it, your progress for each profession, and which zone you're in. When there's nothing left in your current zone, it tells you which portal to take or which zone to head to.

Choosing a look

The first time you log in, a window asks how you want the addon to look. Every window in the addon changes to match. To change it later, open Settings and press Change look, or type /pkt theme.

Warm walnut and brass with engraved borders and corner brackets, like a craftsman's notebook.

Workbench

Flat and modern. Each profession has its own color, and your progress shows as one pip per treasure.

Minimizing while you travel

Press the minimize button in the tracker's header and it shrinks to a slim bar. The bar shows your next treasure, its zone and coordinates, and how many are left.

  • Press the arrow on the bar to skip to the next treasure.
  • Press the expand button to bring back the full tracker.
  • Type /pkt mini to switch between the two.

The bar opens where the tracker was, and the addon remembers whether you left it minimized.

Darkmoon Faire quests

Each month the Darkmoon Faire has a quest worth +2 knowledge for every profession. When you arrive on Darkmoon Island, the helper opens by itself and shows the quests for your professions:

  • Whether each quest is Available or Done this Faire.
  • The vendor's name and roughly where they stand.
  • What to bring with you, and where to get it.
  • What the vendor gives you, so you don't buy it twice.
  • Numbered steps from accepting the quest to turning it in.

Use the Prev and Next buttons at the bottom to switch between your professions. You can also open the helper any time with /pkt dmf or by right-clicking the minimap button.

Step by step

Press the minimize button in the helper's header and it becomes a slim bar showing only the step you're on, like "Step 2 of 5" and what to do. Use the arrows on the bar to move forward or back.

  • Going past the last step moves on to your next profession's quest.
  • Going back from step 1 returns to the previous profession's last step.
  • Your current step is highlighted when you expand the full window.

Your current step is remembered until you reload or log out.

Settings

Open settings with the button in the tracker's header, by middle-clicking the minimap button, or by typing /pkt settings.

Appearance
Shows your current look. Press Change look to pick another.
Waypoint system
Native + TomTom sets both arrows. Native only uses the game's arrow. TomTom only uses TomTom's arrow. None places a map pin with no arrow.
Professions
Press Select manually to pick your professions if they aren't detected.

Tips and FAQ

I'm standing on the spot but there's no treasure.

Some treasures are hidden by phasing until you finish a storyline, quest or achievement, and the tip in the tracker says which one. Several treasures in Atal'Aman only appear after the local storyline.

Two treasures point to the same place.

A few treasures share a spot. In Zul'Aman, the Alchemy and Enchanting treasures sit on the same cart.

The addon moved my quest tracking arrow.

Right after a loading screen or zone change, the addon takes the arrow back for the treasure route. If you track a quest yourself after that, it stays tracked. Press Next, Prev, Nearest or First to point the arrow back at a treasure.

The Darkmoon Faire helper didn't open.

It opens on its own when you arrive on Darkmoon Island. You can open it any time with /pkt dmf or by right-clicking the minimap button.

How often do the Darkmoon Faire quests reset?

Every month, when the Faire returns. Each one gives +2 knowledge for that profession.
