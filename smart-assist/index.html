User guide

SmartAssist

Never edit your Misdirection, Tricks of the Trade or Power Infusion macro again. SmartAssist builds the macro for you and keeps it pointed at the right player, switching to your tank automatically when you join a group.

Download on CurseForge Source on GitHub
  • Command: /smartassist
  • Game: Retail, Midnight
  • Classes: Hunter, Rogue, Priest

Preview

Features

  • Builds your macro for you: one macro for Misdirection, Tricks of the Trade or Power Infusion, with the spell's icon, kept up to date automatically.
  • Finds the tank: when you join a group or the group changes, it points the macro at your tank.
  • Quick target menu: hover the minimap button to switch the macro to your tank, target, target of target, focus or pet in one click.
  • Sensible fallback: if the player you chose isn't there, the macro falls back to your pet, then to a normal cast.
  • Reminds you when you zone in: if you're in a group with no target set, the quick menu pops up.
  • Combat safe: changes you make in combat are applied as soon as combat ends.
  • Warns you if the spell is missing: if you aren't talented into the spell, the quick menu says so.
  • Three looks: Workbench, Artisan's Ledger and Lixard Classic, the same looks as my other addons.

Getting started

  1. Install the addon and log in on your Hunter, Rogue or Priest.
  2. The addon creates a macro named after your spell, such as "Misdirection".
  3. Open your Macros window (/macro) and drag the macro onto your action bar.
  4. Join a group. The macro now points at your tank, and the minimap button shows who it's set to. Priests choose their target instead (see Classes).

Macro slots full? The addon needs one free general macro slot. If all 18 are used, it tells you in chat. Delete one and log in again.

Choosing a target

Hover the minimap button to open the quick menu. The bottom of the menu shows who the macro is set to now.

OptionWhat it does
Set to Party Tank / Raid TankPoint the macro at your group's tank. Hunters and Rogues, while you're in a group.
Save Target's NamePriests only. Lock the macro onto the player you're targeting right now.
Set to TargetPoint it at whoever you're targeting when you press the macro.
Set to Target of TargetPoint it at your target's target.
Set to FocusPoint it at your focus target.
Set to PetPoint it at your pet. Hunters only, while a pet is out.
Clear TargetRemove the target so the spell casts normally.

When finding the tank, the addon checks for a player set as main tank first, then anyone in the tank role, then the tank-capable player with the most health.

Classes

Hunter: Misdirection

Starts pointed at your pet. Switches to the tank when you join a group, and back to your pet when you leave.

Rogue: Tricks of the Trade

Starts pointed at your focus target. Switches to the tank when you join a group.

Priest: Power Infusion

Power Infusion goes on a damage dealer, not the tank, so there's no automatic tank switching. Target the player you want and choose Save Target's Name, or set the macro to your target, target of target or focus.

Tips and FAQ

The quick menu won't open.

It can't open during combat, and entering combat closes it. Open it again once combat ends.

It says "No tank found in group".

Nobody in your group is set as a tank or can tank. Pick a target from the quick menu instead.

The macro has a question-mark icon.

You don't know the spell yet. Once you learn it, the macro uses the real icon.

Which classes does it support?

Hunter, Rogue and Priest. On other classes the addon stays quiet.

I used Mark of the Tank. What changed?

Mark of the Tank is now called SmartAssist. The command is /smartassist instead of /mott, and your settings start fresh. Your macro keeps its name, so your action bar button keeps working. If a MOTT folder is still in your AddOns folder, delete it, or both addons will fight over the same macro.
