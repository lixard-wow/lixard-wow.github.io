SmartAssist

SmartAssist builds your Misdirection, Tricks of the Trade or Power Infusion macro and keeps it aimed at the right player. Join a group and it switches to your tank on its own.

Features

Your macro, kept up to date

  • Builds the macro for you. One macro for your spell, with the spell's icon, made the first time you log in.
  • Rewrites it when you pick someone new. You never open the macro window, and your action bar button keeps working.
  • Never wastes the cast. If the player you picked isn't there or is dead, Hunters fall back to their pet, then everyone falls back to a normal cast on your target.
  • Safe in combat. Macros can't change during combat, so anything you pick mid-fight is applied as soon as combat ends.
  • Tells you if the spell is missing. If you haven't learned the spell, the quick menu says so and the macro shows a question mark until you do.

Finds your tank

  • Hunters and Rogues: when you join a group, or the group changes, the macro points at the tank.
  • Hunters: when you leave the group, it switches back to your pet.
  • Zone in with your group and no target set, and the quick menu pops up so you can pick one.

Supported classes

ClassSpellStarts aimed at
HunterMisdirectionYour pet
RogueTricks of the TradeYour focus
PriestPower InfusionNobody, until you pick a player

Power Infusion goes on a damage dealer, so Priests don't get automatic tank switching. Instead, pick a player and their name goes straight into the macro.

Five looks

Click the gear button at the top of any SmartAssist window to switch between Workbench, Artisan Ledger, Horde, Alliance and Slate (which uses your class color as its accent). Your choice is saved for each character.

Installing

  1. The easy way: open the CurseForge app, search for SmartAssist and click Install.
  2. By hand: download the zip from CurseForge, or from GitHub (github.com/lixard-wow/SmartAssist: Code > Download ZIP).
  3. Copy the SmartAssist folder into World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns.
  4. Restart the game the first time, so it finds the new addon.

Getting started

  1. Log in on your Hunter, Rogue or Priest. On other classes the addon stays quiet.
  2. The setup guide opens a few seconds after you log in. It shows once for each character.
  3. Choose how it behaves: whether it finds the tank on its own, who the macro is aimed at, and whether to show the minimap button.
  4. Choose where the macro is saved: your character's own macro tab (recommended) or General Macros, shared by all your characters.
  5. Drag the icon from the guide onto your action bar, or click it and then click a slot. The guide tells you when it finds the macro on your bars.
  6. Join a group. The macro now points at your tank. Priests pick a damage dealer from the quick menu instead.

The quick menu

Hover the minimap button to open the quick menu. The bottom line shows who the macro is aimed at right now. The menu can't open during combat.

OptionWhat it does
Set to Party Tank / Set to Raid TankAim the macro at your group's tank. Hunters and Rogues, while in a group.
Set to namePriests in a party: one line for each damage dealer. Click one to put their name in the macro.
Save Target's NamePriests: put the name of the player you're targeting into the macro.
Set to TargetAim it at whoever you have targeted when you press it.
Set to Target of TargetAim it at your target's target.
Set to FocusAim it at your focus.
Set to PetHunters, while a pet is out.
Clear TargetRemove the target so the spell casts normally.

To find the tank, SmartAssist looks for a player set as Main Tank first, then anyone in the Tank role, then the tank-capable player with the most health.

Tips and FAQ

The quick menu won't open.

It can't open during combat, and entering combat closes it. Hover the minimap button again once combat ends.

It says "No tank found in group".

Nobody in your group is set as a tank or can tank. Pick a target from the quick menu instead.

The macro has a question-mark icon.

You haven't learned the spell yet. Once you take the talent, the macro uses the real icon and works on its own.

It says my macro tab is full.

The macro needs one free slot in the tab it's saved in. Delete a macro there, or click Switch in the settings to save it in the other tab.

I picked a new target in combat, but nothing changed.

Macros can't change during combat. Your choice is applied as soon as combat ends.

I used Mark of the Tank. What changed?

Mark of the Tank is now called SmartAssist, and the command is /smartassist instead of /mott. Your settings start fresh, but your macro keeps its name, so your action bar button keeps working. If a MOTT folder is still in your AddOns folder, delete it, or the two will fight over the same macro.
