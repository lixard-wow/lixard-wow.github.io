Trailmark: what other guides have

A survey of the leveling and quest-guide addons for WoW Forever (and the big Classic ones players compare against), from their public pages only. Below: what is left to decide or build, everything already done (folded away), the other addons, and the features from them Trailmark still does not have.

What's left

Everything not built yet, with what we decided.

FeatureDecisionWhy
Forever's own factionsNeeds in-game data21 new factions (Emerald Wardens, Kirin Tor, Powderfuse and more) have no data anywhere yet. Trailmark could log where your rep comes from while you play and build guides from that.
PvP faction guidesMaybeWarsong, Arathi Basin and Alterac Valley reputation; mostly battleground play, little to route.
Mount guidesMaybeRep mounts are already in the reputation rewards; a separate guide would add class mounts and drops.
Hardcore modeMaybeCheap as a "safer" switch (hide group and red quests, warn on dangerous elites) if you or your players run Hardcore; skip otherwise.
TreasuresMaybeRares are done; treasure chests need their own spawn data.
Puzzle pictures, full quest textMaybeHow-to tips already cover most tricky quests.
TranslationsLaterAll text is in language keys (0.20.1); adding a language is one file.
Walking-path arrowLaterNo pathfinding for addons; needs hand-made paths and lots of in-game testing. The recorder could collect paths first.
Own quest trackerSkippedThe guide window already shows the current objectives; Blizzard's tracker covers the rest.
In-game guide editorSkippedThe recorder and route codes cover sharing; routes are written from game data.
Gamepad supportSkippedNot needed.
Anonymous route timing, desktop clientSkippedNeeds an outside server or app.

Known gaps in what is built

Parts of finished features that still have holes, mostly from missing data.

WhereGap
Profession plansTailoring 271-300 has no plan (no trainer or vendor pattern with common materials). Some Forever recipe learn levels are estimated.
DungeonsForever-only dungeons are left out (Excavation Site: Wetlands, The Drowned City and others): sources disagree.
Class questsChains that need dungeons or zig-zag across the map are left out (Whirlwind, Succubus, Call of Water).
Boats and docksSeveral dock spots are estimates and say so on the waypoint; no boat timetables.
Nearest serviceClassic NPC data; Forever additions are missing and mailboxes are thin (59 in the data).
Exploration11 small areas have no map piece and may never count as explored; capital cities have no areas.
ReputationClassic reputation rates; Human bonus and shared city/town reputation are not shown.
Zephras IsleAbout 30 side-quest steps per faction have no quest giver yet; a recorder run on a Skyborne character would fill them.

  • Share quests with my party when you pick them up, optional (0.18.3)
  • Waypoint choice: Trailmark, Blizzard/WaypointUI/LixardUI or TomTom, with a themed in-world marker and an optional screen arrow (0.19.0)
  • Steps-done line where step numbers jump, click to show them (0.22.1)

  • Exploration guide: 44 zones, 577 areas, Guide me walks you through the missing ones (0.21.0)
  • Language keys for every text, ready to translate (0.20.1)

The other addons

Polestar Guides is the one to beat: free, open source, the most downloads of any Forever guide and the most features. Quest Pilot, RestedXP and Zygor are the other serious rivals; RestedXP and Zygor charge for most of their Forever routes.
