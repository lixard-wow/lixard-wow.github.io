Claude Rework high confidence

Cat leveling build that now takes Heart of the Wild and Naturalist (10% all damage) in the 40s instead of Thick Hide, Furor and extra Shredding Attacks.

Talents. Every post-launch feral source (Icy Veins, Maxroll and Wowhead via Timesaver, WoWForeverBuilds) spends 10 points on Heart of the Wild plus Naturalist, because Naturalist is a flat 10% to all damage and Heart of the Wild adds 10% Strength in Cat Form; Trailmark had no Naturalist points. Feral Instinct replaces Thick Hide: both build sources take it, and the 30% Swipe bonus is what bear AoE pulls need before 20. Feral Charge is taken by both for the bear interrupt and gap closer. Lacerate gives the 25th point before Rend and Tear and a bleed for Rend and Tear in bear. Furor 5 and the third Shredding Attacks point are dropped; Icy Veins calls Furor low value and Warcraft Tavern skips Shredding for solo play.

Order. Ferocity 5 first makes Maul and Claw cheaper while you are still in Bear Form at 10-19 (Icy Veins and Trailmark agree; Maxroll opens with Furor). Feral Swiftness and Feral Instinct finish row 2 before Cat Form at 20, and Feral Charge at 20 is the first row-3 point. Predatory Strikes from 25, Leader of the Pack at 30 (earliest possible), Rend and Tear 35-39 and Shifting Power at 40 match Icy Veins and Maxroll. Heart of the Wild 41-45 and Naturalist 46-50 come next, then Genesis for Rake and Rip, then Nature's Majesty and the hit from Nature's Reach. Primal Bite is the last point because a cat leveler only uses it when tanking a dungeon.

Versus Trailmark. Removed Thick Hide 3, Furor 5, Shredding Attacks 3. Added Feral Instinct 3, Feral Charge 1, Lacerate 1, Naturalist 5, Primal Bite 1. Feral Swiftness moved to 15-16; Heart of the Wild moved ahead of Genesis.

Method offers both raid crit and dungeon Enrage variants; this is a balanced shield baseline rather than a proven best build for every raid.

Order. Learn Vanguard at 29 after the early Defense and threat ranks. Focused Rage and Concussion Blow open Bastion at 35, then Shield Slam arrives at 40. Deflection fills 41-45. The fifteen-point Fury dip fills 46-60, ending with Enrage after the ten-point gate is met.

Versus Trailmark. Enrage: 0 to 5; Improved Bloodrage: 2 to 0; Improved Shield Bash: 1 to 0; Improved Shield Wall: 2 to 1; Improved Sunder Armor: 3 to 0; Improved Thunder Clap: 3 to 0; Unbridled Wrath: 0 to 5. Pick order revised as explained in order_why. Note revised.
